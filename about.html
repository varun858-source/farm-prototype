ABOUT MAATI · OUR MISSION & TRUST CHARTER

We build plainspoken field and herd tools so a 2-acre family farm has the same clarity as a 2,000-acre estate.

Maati exists for working farmers, dairy cooperative members, and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs). We started with a simple observation: rural families do not run separate businesses for their soil, their crops, their buffaloes, and their mandi sales — yet agricultural software kept forcing them into disconnected, English-only apps built for urban desktops.

Every instrument we build must pass two tests before it ships: First, can a farmer use it with one thumb in bright midday sun on a ₹6,000 phone over patchy 2G/3G? Second, does it state its uncertainty honestly rather than pretending a computer screen knows more than the person standing in the soil?

THE MAATI FIELD CHARTER

Four rules we never compromise on

PRINCIPLE 01 · DATA OWNERSHIP

Your plot and herd records belong to you

A farmer’s land boundary, yield history, and dairy ledger are their personal livelihood asset. We never sell farm-level data to commodity speculators or agro-chemical marketers. Credit and insurance packets are shared only when the farmer explicitly approves via SMS passkey.

PRINCIPLE 02 · DATA HONESTY

We show confidence ranges, never fake certainty

Agriculture deals with living soil and shifting monsoons. Whether we are diagnosing leaf blight (e.g., 94.2% match) or projecting wheat harvest (18.6–20.2 quintals/acre), we always display the confidence interval and underlying inputs plainly.

PRINCIPLE 03 · ZERO INPUT KICKBACKS

Unbiased biological and generic chemical advice

We do not take commission from pesticide or feed manufacturers. Every disease diagnosis lists cultural and biological remedies first, and generic active-ingredient dosages (approved by state agricultural universities) second.

PRINCIPLE 04 · RADICAL ACCESSIBILITY

Voice, WhatsApp, and 2G SMS are first-class citizens

Every advisory and alert feature works in 12 Indian languages across spoken voice calls, compressed WhatsApp threads, and plain 2G SMS — so literacy or bandwidth never locks a family out.
