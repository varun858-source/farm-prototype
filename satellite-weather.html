FEATURE 01 / 04 · ORBITAL CHLOROPHYLL VIGOR ANALYSIS

NDVI Crop Stress Mapping & Zone Legend

Rather than overwhelming farmers with raw false-color satellite imagery, Maati translates Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) reflectance into a plain 3-zone field grid: Healthy Canopy, Watch / Mild Stress, and Critical Stress Zone.

ZONE A · NDVI 0.65 TO 0.85

Healthy Vigorous Canopy

Active chlorophyll photosynthesis and full row closure. No additional spray or booster needed.

ZONE B · NDVI 0.40 TO 0.64

Stressed / Watch Zone

Early moisture dip or mild foliar nitrogen slowdown. Inspect drip lateral pressure or border wind rows.

ZONE C · NDVI BELOW 0.40

Critical Stress Zone

Severe root waterlogging, emitter blockage, or pest hotspot. Queued automatically for scout & drone pass.

Clear night skies and calm north winds will drop ground-level temperature near freezing between 2:00 AM and 5:30 AM Thursday.

REQUIRED FIELD ACTION TODAY:

REQUIRED FIELD ACTION TODAY:
