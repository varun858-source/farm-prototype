Crop Disease & Pest Detection from Leaf Photos

When a fungal blight or borer hits a plot, waiting a week for an input dealer visit often costs 25% of the harvest. Farmers snap a close-up photo of the affected leaf, stem, or boll — even offline — and receive an immediate diagnosis with locally approved organic and chemical dosages.

STEP 01

Snap Leaf Photo in Field

Take 1–2 close-up photos of the top and underside of the affected leaf using any smartphone camera.

STEP 02

Lesion & Pattern Check

Our model compares lesion rings, vein yellowing, and pest frass against 420,000+ agronomist-labeled Indian field photos.

STEP 03

Honest Confidence Score

Displays the exact match percentage and flags if a second photo or FPO agronomist call is recommended.

STEP 04

Local Dosage & Follow-Up

Provides biological/organic controls first, exact gram-per-liter chemical dosage second, and a 7-day recovery check.

INPUT 02

Tracks cumulative rainfall, growing degree-days (GDD), and flowering-stage heat or dry-spell stress.

INPUT 03

Soil Health Analysis: NPK, Moisture % & pH Trends

Most farmers apply urea by habit rather than soil need. Maati supports two practical input paths — start immediately with smartphone topsoil photos paired with your government Soil Health Card, or connect a solar LoRaWAN root probe for continuous 15-minute readings.

INPUT PATH A (NO HARDWARE)

Soil Photo + Card

Snap a wet/dry topsoil photo beside a coin and upload your block Soil Health Card for texture, organic carbon, and moisture estimation.

INPUT PATH B (CONNECTED)

3-Depth Sensor Probe

Push-in solar LoRaWAN probe reads Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium (NPK), volumetric moisture %, and pH at 15cm & 30cm root depths.

SEASON TRACKING

16-Week Trend Line

Watches nutrient drawdown after each irrigation or rain event so leaching and alkalinity lockup are caught early.

FIELD OUTPUT

Split Fertilizer Plan

Recommends exact kg/acre of Urea, DAP, MOP, or farmyard compost for the next crop stage — cutting excess fertilizer waste.
