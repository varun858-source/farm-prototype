Cattle Health & Heat-Cycle Monitoring

Before a cow or buffalo shows visible fever or a drop in the milk bucket, her cud-chewing (rumination) minutes fall and her standing bouts shift. Whether you use acoustic neck collars or a single solar camera in the cattle shed, Maati translates behavior into a simple animal-status list: Healthy, Watch, or Alert.

STEP 01

Collar or Shed Camera

Tracks chew count, head movement, trough visits, and mounting behavior 24 hours a day.

STEP 02

Per-Animal Baseline

Learns each animal’s normal rumination (460–520 min/day) adjusted for breed and ambient heat.

STEP 03

Illness & Estrus Flags

Distinguishes between restlessness from a heat cycle (estrus) and lethargy from early fever or mastitis.

STEP 04

Plain Status List

Sends a clear WhatsApp/SMS alert with the exact time window for Artificial Insemination (AI) or vet check.

Milk Yield & Quality Prediction

Dairy societies pay by both volume and Fat / SNF (Solid-Not-Fat) percentage. Maati ingests daily cooperative collection slips or inline parlor sensors, combines them with lactation day and ambient temperature, and forecasts your 14-day yield and quality score.

INPUT 01

Sensor or Society Slip

Reads morning & evening liters, Fat %, SNF %, and milk conductivity automatically.

INPUT 02

Lactation & Weather Model

Accounts for days-in-milk curve, pregnancy status, and night temperature humidity index (THI).

OUTPUT

14-Day Yield & Quality Score

Projects daily liters and Fat/SNF payout band per animal or for the whole milking herd.

LIVESTOCK USP 03 / 03 · LEAST-COST BALANCED NUTRITION

Automated Feed Optimization

Feed accounts for 65% to 70% of the cost of producing a liter of milk. Most dairy farmers either over-spend on commercial bags or under-feed essential minerals. Maati takes your animal’s weight, breed, and production goal and builds a balanced daily ration from the dry stalks, green fodder, and oilcakes already in your village.

STEP 01

Weight, Breed & Stage

Select breed (Murrah, Gir, Sahiwal, HF Cross), body weight, current milk liters, and pregnancy month.

STEP 02

Select Available Fodder

Tick the crop residues (wheat bhusa, jowar kadbi) and oilcakes (cottonseed, groundnut, mustard) you have on hand.

STEP 03

Before / After Ration Mix

Balances Crude Protein (CP), Total Digestible Nutrients (TDN), and Calcium-Phosphorus at the lowest daily cost.
